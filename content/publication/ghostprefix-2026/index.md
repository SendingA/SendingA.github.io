abstract: >-
  GhostPrefix investigates reusable audio prefixes that manipulate automatic speech
  recognition without knowing future speech or synchronizing with its waveform.
  The framework combines token-level and representation-level objectives to support
  semantic steering, targeted transcription, and transcription degradation.
  Evaluations cover 13 ASR models from four families in digital and over-the-air
  settings, with downstream effects examined in voice assistants and AI note-taking tools.
